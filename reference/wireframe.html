Modeling notes baked into this map — (1) Each phone counts one person’s shift; the manager merges by station + time, so handoffs stitch together without anyone swapping phones. (2) Tips stay a single station bucket (all to the Special Kids Rodeo); per-person numbers are a bragging-rights estimate from each phone’s exact people-served. (3) Beer is excluded from inventory/orders (kegs, auto-restocked); it still appears on the drink grid. (4) Every person-served tap is timestamped, shown in 15- or 30-min blocks. (5) Boot screen tracks numbers only — the visual boot lives in your file. (6) The team recap writes a text from the stats you pick and opens your Messages app — nothing sends automatically. · Wireframe v2.2
